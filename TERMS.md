# ModWingman terms

ModWingman is provided as a Reddit Devvit app for moderator convenience.
By installing it on your subreddit you accept the following:

1. The summaries written by ModWingman are LLM-generated and may be wrong.
   They are advisory, not authoritative. Moderators retain full
   discretion over ban-appeal decisions.

2. Message bodies of appeal-triggering modmail are sent to Google AI Studio
   (Gemini 2.5 Flash) for summarisation. Do not install ModWingman if your
   subreddit's policies disallow this.

3. No SLA. The app may stop working at any time. Open an issue at
   https://github.com/run58669-maker/modwingman if you encounter problems.

4. Use at your own risk.
